CrudeMaps — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 June 2026

CrudeMaps is designed to respect your privacy. In short: it does not collect, store off your device, or transmit any personal information.

Location

CrudeMaps reads your GPS location from your Garmin device only to show your current position on the map, on the watch itself. Your location is never saved off the device, sent to us or to any third party, or used for any other purpose.

Network use

CrudeMaps includes an optional map-download feature that can fetch additional map tiles over the internet (relayed through your phone). This feature is turned off in this release, so the app makes no network connections at all. Even when the feature is enabled, only map-tile requests are made — your location and any personal data are never transmitted.

Data we collect

None. CrudeMaps has no analytics, no accounts, and no servers that receive your data. Map data is bundled with the app and derived from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL).

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact: freethebikes@gmail.com

Because the app sends no personal data anywhere, there is nothing to request, export, or delete.
